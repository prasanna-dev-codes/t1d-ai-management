# Dataset strategy and provenance register

Status: No datasets acquired, downloaded, licensed, or inspected. Candidate names below are research leads from the project scope, not verified access or fitness claims. Exact source URLs/releases, contents, sizes, license terms, redistribution rights, consent scope, and present availability are **Verification required**. No participant-level records belong in Git, including de-identified research records.

## Candidate register
### Food image classification — Food-101 candidate
Source lead: official Food-101 dataset/research publisher; exact release and rights **Verification required**. Intended use: baseline single-food classification, followed by separate meal-domain evaluation. Available-data expectation to verify: labeled food photographs/classes; no assumption of portion mass or nutrient ground truth. Access/license: verify academic/commercial uses, derivative weights, and redistribution separately. Limits: curated classes may not represent mixed Indian meals. Preprocess by decoding, deduplicating, preserving split/source IDs, and fitting augmentation only on training. Leakage: duplicates, near-duplicates and recipe/site correlations across splits; group related images.

### Food segmentation — UECFOODPIX COMPLETE candidate
Source lead: official UEC food-segmentation research distribution; identity, label schema and availability **Verification required**. Intended use: multi-food masks and component recognition. Available-data expectation: images with segmentation annotations, subject to inspection. Access/license: verify permitted research use and annotation rights before acquisition. Limits: regional dish taxonomy, overlap, hidden food and inconsistent ingredient definitions. Preprocess masks with class/version manifests and inspect occlusion labels; never infer mass from mask area alone. Leakage: shared dishes/photos across releases and augmentations crossing splits.

### Portion estimation — Nutrition5k candidate plus controlled synthetic fixtures
Source lead: official Nutrition5k project/research distribution; modalities, release and license **Verification required**. Intended use: compare portion/nutrient estimates using available reference measurements, if authorized. Available data: exact image/video/depth/ingredient/nutrition fields must be inspected rather than assumed complete. Access/license: verify redistribution, attribution, weight training rights and participant/privacy constraints. Limits: imaging geometry, recipes and settings may differ from handheld meals. Preprocess with explicit mass/energy units and retain measurement method. Split by dish/recipe/session to avoid frame leakage. Synthetic fixtures can test plumbing, never establish real-world portion accuracy.

### Indian food composition — ICMR-NIN Indian Food Composition Tables candidate
Source lead: official ICMR-NIN publication/distribution; edition, machine-readable availability and permissions **Verification required**. Intended use: governed nutrient mapping for Indian ingredients/foods. Available fields and coverage must be checked against the actual licensed edition; do not assume a freely redistributable API or cooked-recipe labels. Access/license: verify extraction, database building, redistribution and attribution rights. Limits: preparation, edible fraction, moisture and recipe variation can dominate estimates. Normalize reference basis (for example per recorded edible mass), units, raw/cooked identity and nutrient definitions without inventing missing values. Leakage: using evaluated meal recipes to tune mapping; freeze mappings before test evaluation. Any alternative food database needs the same review and provenance.

### CGM and insulin history — OhioT1DM candidate
Source lead: official OhioT1DM research dataset distributor; release, application process and data-use agreement **Verification required**. Intended use: retrospective glucose forecasting and documented context availability. Available data: confirm CGM, insulin/meal/event fields, timestamps, device metadata and missingness per release; do not assume all modalities are complete. Access/license: obtain legitimate authorization, inspect retention/publication/redistribution restrictions and ethics requirements before acquisition. No exact participant counts, rates or terms asserted here. Limits: cohort/device diversity, missing or late annotations, self-report errors and restricted access. Preprocess per patient with explicit source units, clock alignment, gaps and knowledge-time provenance. Leakage: overlapping windows, same-patient split mixing, future glucose/meal annotations, post-hoc outcomes and pooled normalization.

### Activity context — dataset-dependent authorized streams
Source lead: activity fields in an authorized metabolic study or a separately approved wearable dataset; availability **Verification required**. Intended use: ablation of activity context, not unvalidated causal effects. Available data must be inventoried for timestamps, intensity units, devices and missingness. Access/license/consent: verify linkage rights; do not join unrelated subjects across datasets. Limits: inconsistent wear time and device proxies; missing activity is not inactivity. Preprocess with sensor coverage and units, fit transforms on training only. Leakage: using future activity windows or labels created retrospectively as contemporaneous inputs.

### Insulin events — approved study history and synthetic fixtures
Use authorized study insulin logs only if formulation, amount units, event type and timing can be verified. Source/release/access/license **Verification required** for each study. Synthetic planned/reported/cancelled events cover software tests only. Do not invent active insulin, delivered basal exposure or sensitivity from missing logs. Planned versus delivered events and late corrections are distinct. Preserve uncertainties; prevent target-derived retrospective annotation leakage.

## Acquisition gate and manifest
Before any download, a human researcher records: official source URL, release/date, file digests, owner/contact, license/DUA reference and permitted uses, ethics/consent status, retention/deletion constraints, expected modalities/units, subject pseudonymization strategy and storage permissions. If any required right is unknown, mark blocked and use synthetic fixtures. Record redistribution and trained-weight rights separately. This gate is a future task, not evidence that access is granted.

After acquisition outside Git, inventory exact fields and missingness, audit identifiers/EXIF, validate schema using approved data tools, document exclusions, and freeze a versioned derived manifest. Raw data are read-only; transformations are versioned, deterministic and reproducible. Store only non-identifying manifest templates and aggregate evaluation reports in Git, subject to disclosure review. Dataset payloads, per-patient tables, meal photos, local caches and private DUAs stay in governed storage. Ignore rules alone do not protect data.

## Evaluation separation
Use synthetic fixtures for software contracts; never cite their performance as clinical evidence. Group food images by dish/recipe/source. Split metabolic data by patient for generalization and by time for within-patient personalization, with overlap purging. Exclude outcomes unavailable at each prediction cutoff from features and retrieval. Record absent original knowledge-times as a study limitation. No downloading, model training, dataset-size claims, or permission verification occurs in Phase 00.
