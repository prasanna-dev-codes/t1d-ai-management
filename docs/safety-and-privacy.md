# Safety and privacy specification

Status: Phase 00 research specification preserving existing [repository safety boundaries](../AGENTS.md). Controls below are planned verification obligations, not clinical validation, approved numeric policies, deployment settings, or regulatory compliance. Any later change to clinical safety policy or dosage logic requires explicit human approval; qualified clinical review is additionally required for clinical definitions/protocols.

## Intended use and invariant boundaries
Research, simulation and authorized retrospective evaluation only. No autonomous insulin delivery, unvalidated patient-facing dosing instructions, LLM dosing authority, automatic clinical-parameter changes, or claim that dose clipping guarantees safety. Forecasting stays separate from restricted clinical research simulation. Any insulin-related estimate must be labeled investigational, state uncertainty/assumptions/missing data and require qualified clinician review. No dosage algorithm is specified here.

## Structured risk register
Qualitative severity labels are preliminary research triage, not a validated quantitative risk score. Likelihood is unknown until evidence exists; residual risk is unaccepted pending review in every case.

- **R-001 — Incorrect image/portion/nutrient estimate.** Potential high-severity harm if treated as treatment input; likelihood unknown. Controls: reviewed portions, source versions, uncertainty and explicit experimental labels (FR-002–005, SAFE-005). Evidence: mixed/unknown-food and missing-portion fixtures, reference-measured evaluation, correction usability checks. Owner: Developer B and qualified reviewer. Residual: hidden ingredients/domain shift remain; no dosing use.
- **R-002 — Stale, future, wrong-unit or invalid CGM.** Potential high severity; likelihood unknown. Controls: explicit units, timestamp and source-quality checks, approved freshness/applicability policy, fail closed (SAFE-003). Evidence: boundary/skew/unit/gap tests and device-profile review. Owner: Developer A plus clinician. Residual: undetected sensor error; policy thresholds unapproved.
- **R-003 — Missing/conflicting insulin or clinical context.** Potential high severity; likelihood unknown. Controls: no guessed parameters/active insulin; planned versus delivered distinction; explicit missingness/abstention and settings approval (SAFE-003, SAFE-007). Evidence: missing-context and settings-revision regressions. Owner: Developer A plus qualified clinician. Residual: self-report quality cannot be guaranteed.
- **R-004 — Overconfident/shifted forecast.** Potential high severity; likelihood unknown. Controls: baseline comparisons, held-out calibration, subgroup/shift evaluation and output gates (FR-009–010, SAFE-004). Evidence: risk-coverage reports and out-of-distribution fixtures. Owner: Developer C. Residual: interval coverage may fail under shift; no clinical clearance.
- **R-005 — Cross-patient/future-information retrieval.** Potential high privacy/scientific severity; likelihood unknown. Controls: scope and knowledge-time filters, canonical rechecks, scoped caches (FR-008, NFR-008). Evidence: foreign/future/late-annotation adversarial tests. Owner: Developer A. Residual: inaccurate source availability timestamps must be disclosed.
- **R-006 — Treatment misuse or automation bias.** Potential high severity; likelihood unknown. Controls: no dose/actuation API, research labels, human review, separate simulation approvals (SAFE-001–002, SAFE-006, SAFE-008). Evidence: route/contract/UI review and reviewer comprehension checks. Owner: all developers and supervisor. Residual: users may still misinterpret forecasts; direct participant access remains gated.
- **R-007 — Sensitive-data exposure.** Potential high privacy severity; likelihood unknown. Controls: least privilege, encryption, governed objects, redaction, consent-bound processing, no records in Git (NFR-002, SAFE-010). Evidence: access tests, log/trace leak tests, secret/artifact scanning. Owner: Developer A. Residual: backups, exports and third-party processing require separate review.
- **R-008 — Corrupt/stale artifacts or partial failures.** Potential high severity; likelihood unknown. Controls: immutable digests, revision checks, idempotency, protected audit and dependency failure abstention (NFR-009, SAFE-004). Evidence: tamper/outage/retry/recovery tests. Owner: Developer C with A. Residual: operational recovery/settings not yet approved.
- **R-009 — Unsafe uploads or malicious retrieved instructions.** Potential security/clinical severity high; likelihood unknown. Controls: bounded safe decoding, content validation, untrusted-content handling, no executable LLM authority. Evidence: malformed-media and prompt-injection fixtures. Owner: Developer B. Residual: decoder/provider vulnerabilities require ongoing review.
- **R-010 — Unapproved protocol or regulatory claim.** Potential high severity; likelihood unknown. Controls: approved protocol/settings lineage, research-only scope, explicit expert review (SAFE-006–008). Evidence: role/approval denial tests and claim review. Owner: supervisor/qualified reviewer. Residual: legal intended-use classification remains unresolved.

## Deterministic eligibility and missing data
Validate schema and identity before processing; explicit units, finite values, timestamps, device/source status, model feature compatibility, required context, consent and approved policy before inference; output finiteness, uncertainty/applicability and complete lineage before research display. Missing policy or required inputs must not produce an eligible result. No assumed carbohydrate amount, sensitivity, carbohydrate ratio, active insulin, target glucose, unit or treatment parameter. Unknown nutrition is not zero; absent activity is not inactivity. Optional covariates are permitted only by the evaluated feature schema with documented missingness handling.

Freshness compares the operation cutoff to the latest valid source reading and required history coverage, considering timezone normalization, gaps, duplicate/out-of-order delivery, device status and clock skew. No universal freshness duration, glucose range, emergency threshold or uncertainty cutoff is supplied here. These are versioned policy proposals to be approved by qualified review before the feature becomes eligible. Historical storage and current-prediction eligibility are distinct.

Abstain on missing/invalid required context, stale inputs, unsupported population/device, unavailable canonical validation/audit, unapproved/incompatible model, uncertain or nonfinite output, missing policy or revoked consent. Invalid schema is rejected; valid but unreliable research inputs abstain with stable reason codes, assumptions and missing-data list. Never substitute a number or suppressed warning. Repeat gates for workers, cached results, exports and direct API calls. Human acknowledgement cannot waive deterministic gates.

## Human oversight and emergency handling
Display experimental status, uncertainty meaning, lineage, assumptions, missing data and limitations alongside results. Qualified clinician review is mandatory for insulin-related research estimates. Clinical settings require explicit approved immutable revisions and audit; models/LLMs cannot approve or write them. Simulation protocols require independent approval, restricted roles and no actuation path.

Emergency messaging must remain conservative and direct people to established emergency services/clinical care when appropriate, with qualified review of final wording and localization before UI implementation. This specification does not define patient-specific emergency thresholds or treatment instructions. Prototype availability or model output must never be presented as emergency coverage.

## Consent, access, minimization and lifecycle
No real-world health ingestion until applicable governance, ethics, lawful basis/consent, intended-use restrictions and access/retention plans are reviewed. Store minimal pseudonymous research references; keep re-identification keys outside the repository and research domain. Pseudonymization is not anonymization. Study grants plus patient scope apply to every read/write, object URL, job, graph traversal, vector candidate and audit query. Use least-privilege administrative roles; privileged access and grants require traceability.

Encrypt transport and data at rest in approved environments; manage keys separately with rotation/access controls. Never log medical payloads, tokens, images or raw prompts. Audit metadata remains sensitive and has a protected access path distinct from operational logs. Backups need encryption, access restrictions and synthetic restoration tests. No hosted telemetry, external model upload or cross-border transfer is authorized by naming a service.

Define retention by data category (raw images, events, derived indexes, audit, backups, model/training artifacts) with responsible owner, lawful basis, deletion mechanism and verification evidence before acquisition. No retention durations are selected. Withdrawal should block new processing, invalidate caches/index entries and trigger governed deletion/retention review including backups and training influence. Record legal holds or research exceptions only after expert review; do not promise immediate deletion from immutable backups or trained models without evidence.

## Security review and incident readiness
Threat-model broken object authorization, credential theft, malicious media, dataset poisoning, unsafe deserialization, untrusted graph content, dependency compromise, telemetry leakage and artifact tampering. Review signed access to objects, safe content limits, CSRF/session behavior, rate limits, encrypted secrets and audit tamper evidence before integration. A later incident plan needs owner/escalation, access revocation, containment, preservation of protected evidence and expert determination of notification obligations. Phase 00 configures none of these deployment controls.

## Expert-review questions and evidence gates
Applicable jurisdiction, study setting, intended users and consent model are undecided. Expert review must determine medical-device/software classification, research ethics requirements, applicable privacy law (including relevant Indian or other jurisdictional rules), data residency/transfer restrictions, provider contracts and permitted clinical claims. No certification, exemption or compliance conclusion is asserted.

Before clinical feature implementation: approve operational safety policy/thresholds and test matrix. Before simulation: approve protocol, uncertainty assumptions, reviewer authority and output restrictions. Before real data: establish access/ethics/license/governance evidence. Before deployment: approve threat model, controls, synthetic recovery evidence and exact settings. These gates preserve the existing policy; they do not grant approval themselves.

## Phase 02 implementation evidence

The synthetic event slice enforces explicit units/timezones, deterministic validation, append-only revisions, current consent/grants and forced patient-scoped RLS under a non-owner runtime role. Model/derived inputs are quarantined; planned insulin remains historical plan metadata and is never interpreted as administration. No settings/dosing/safety-policy execution or real enrollment exists. These are engineering controls, not clinical validation.

Operational logs/errors omit medical values, URLs and credentials; protected audits store operation/reference metadata. Failure audits use a separate transaction after rollback, with a redacted availability event if the database cannot record them. Encryption at rest, production IdP, regulated audit export, erasure policy, backup restoration and clinical deployment are unverified prerequisites. See [governance](data-governance.md) and [review](phase-02-review.md) for trust boundaries and exact local tests.
